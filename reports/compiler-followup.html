What these experiments establish

The revision fixes selected event references and duration constraints, recovers some missing predicate frames, and removes the earlier nested-case inference timeout. The full compiler accepts 2,108 of 2,375 inputs, up from 2,042. All previous accepted inputs remain accepted; 66 more compile.

The meaning gain remains small. On 20 claims from sources absent from saved earlier reviews, the assistant rates 12 revised outputs faithful, versus 11 previously. The revised sample also has 4 partial,2 incorrect and 2 refused. Two faithful outputs are nominal descriptions. In a separate sample of 10 newly accepted claims, only 2 are faithful,7 are partial and 1 is incorrect. The additional acceptance does not justify declaring the compiler reliable. The separate frozen-model review does not confirm the assistant’s gain: faithful ratings fall from 18 to 17. Its missed omissions and refusal mistakes also make it unsuitable as gold.

The 10 newly accepted cases were randomly selected after freezing, from the 66 additions, excluding the reserved sample. They contain 2 faithful,7 partial and 1 incorrect outputs. The partial outputs omit an exact time, parenthetical detail, another-person distinction or shared temporal scope. The incorrect output misses the obligation in “having to take turns.” This targeted slice supports keeping frame recovery experimental.

DeepProbLog execution: what changed

The previous encoder combined unbound state variables using a truth-table join at each logical node. The new encoder emits separate rules for supported, refuted and unknown. For an AND node, support requires both children; refutation can be established by either child. The remaining rules cover unknown outcomes. N below is the parent node and L/R are its child nodes.

node(N,supported) :- node(L,supported), node(R,supported).
node(N,refuted) :- node(L,refuted).
node(N,refuted) :- node(R,refuted).

This changes the generated program, not the three-state meaning. Exact ProbLog inference still combines overlapping proofs, and repeated observation IDs remain one categorical random variable. Distinct local observations still carry the earlier independence assumption. No zero/one probability pruning is introduced; neural outputs retain their gradient.

Proposed next steps and intended goals
  1. Fix nominal media descriptions and quoted labels — stop footage descriptions becoming world events and label words becoming predicates.
  2. Define refusal conditions or explicit constraints for thresholds, nominal OR, omitted modifiers and shared times — reduce accepted outputs that silently lose meaning.
  3. Obtain human reference judgments for a bounded new-source sample, including reviewer disagreements — validate the evaluation criteria before claiming semantic reliability.
These proposals are not implemented. Keep media verification and relation training deferred. The accepted paper direction is unchanged; sentence rewriting remains unimplemented.
