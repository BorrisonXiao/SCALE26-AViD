How difficult claims are compiled

28 simple demos: what went wrong, what the compiler changes, and what the resulting conditions mean.

Current full run: 1866 pass the checks; 2103 have executable candidates. Passing compilation does not establish that a claim is true.

The parser supplies grammatical links and semantic roles: who did what to whom. The compiler keeps those parts and their scope—whether an event is shown, planned, investigated or discussed. It then writes predicates, small statements that evidence functions can score.
