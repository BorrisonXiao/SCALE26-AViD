      When to stop compiler optimization

      My recommendation is to freeze the current grammar rules as the comparison baseline now. Keep correcting basic source-alignment, quotation-protection and validation bugs, but stop adding an open-ended rule for each new sentence shape. The last batch recovered 58 checks, so we have not measured a diminishing-return curve. The reason to move on is the paper goal and the mix of remaining structural problems, not a claim that the compiler is complete.

      Rewriting can simplify a sentence while retaining its information. It cannot safely invent a missing subject, resolve an ambiguous antecedent from world knowledge, change a quote, drop a modal or make unsupported quantified absence valid. In particular, no audio/no narration requires correct existence-and-negation scope; replacing it with a positive audio entity plus absent is not an established solution.

      Proposed rewrite pilot—before new DeepProbLog experiments

      1. Keep the compiler fixed. Compare original claims with minimally rewritten claims under the same predicates and checks.
      2. Use 40 development cases: 24 failed targets, eight unchanged controls and eight ambiguity/unsupported-semantic guards. Select targets with a reviewed missing value or relationship; exclude overstrict-only alerts from semantic-recovery counts. Use one frozen text model on the claim and existing text parses; exclude truth labels and media observations. Allow an unchanged result or explicit refusal. Reserve source groups before prompt tuning.
      3. Review original → rewrite → predicates. Check who did what to whom, shared participants, quantities with their owners, dates, tense/aspect, polarity, modality, exact quotations and source scope. Repeating a sentence into several clauses must not lose shared identity.
      4. Use proposed development gates: at least 12 of 24 failed targets become reviewed faithful compiled outputs; no unchanged-control regressions; zero accepted critical information changes. An increased syntax-pass count alone is insufficient.
      5. Then freeze the complete preparation pipeline. Proceed to relation learning and DeepProbLog inference/training on checked compiled examples, retaining refusals. The 1,924 automatic passes are not a clean training set.

      These are proposed gates, not results or a statistical accuracy guarantee. At the time of this audit the rewrite layer was unimplemented. The subsequent pilot has its own results tab. This audit is development context, not held-out rewrite evidence.

      Decision

      Do not enable automatic rewriting or scale it to the remaining corpus: the revised pilot fails the predeclared gates.

      The revised prompt improves local recoveries but also adds unsupported assertions. Two of24 targets yield reviewed faithful structures, plus one exact-sign-quotation guard. Ten targets pass automatic checks, which is not10 semantic recoveries. Two proposed controls regress; review rejects them. The initial prompt had one different faithful target recovery; results are not combined per claim.

      The proposed development gates were at least 12/24 faithful recoveries, zero passing-control regressions and zero accepted critical changes. These small-set gates do not establish statistical reliability. Surface checks reject obvious name, number, quotation and modality changes. They cannot detect all role swaps, lost tense or changed scope. Acceptance requires a saved review of the original claim, rewrite and predicates.

      A full rerun with only the three reviewed diagnostic-prompt replacements yields 2,105 generated programs and 1,927 passing content checks across 2,375 claims; 448 still fail strict compilation. This is a diagnostic corpus with three reviewed replacements, not a corpus of 1,927 certified faithful claims. The original-text baseline remains 2,103/1,924. No unreviewed rewrite or initial-prompt success is added to this result.

      Failure patterns
      • Diagnostic text encourages repairs but can also prompt invented signers, mission context and comparative references.
      • The model sometimes claims a rewrite while emitting the exact original; its explanations are not evidence of successful repair.
      • Reparsing can recover a missing participant while assigning another role incorrectly, such as sweep as the kicking actor or track as a running actor.
      • Word coverage misses typed-quantity errors: a rate interval can become object-count bounds, and a fractional orbit count can remain exactly three.
      • Equivalent English negation does not repair the compiler's unsupported quantified-absence scope.
      • Exact names and quotations still change despite explicit instructions; surface checks and review reject those proposals.
