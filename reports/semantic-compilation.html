Meaning preservation in claim compilation

One SRL/dependency compiler, explicit references and source contexts, with a separate check on 20 fresh claims.

What improved, and what remains unresolved

The revised compiler preserves more source contexts and shared references. On the 20 fresh claims reserved before changes, faithful outputs rise from 7 to 8, incorrect outputs fall from 4 to 2, partial outputs rise from 4 to 5, and refusals remain 5. No previously faithful fresh output regressed. This is a modest descriptive gain on a small assistant review, not a semantic accuracy estimate.

The larger improvement on the 24 development cases—7 to 13 faithful outputs—reflects the cases used to design the changes. Across all 2,375 inputs, compilation rises from 1,997 to 2,042. Compilation acceptance is separate from meaning preservation. Accepted programs still contain wrong actors, unresolved references and untyped durations.

Changes to the compilation rules

Table 1. Bounded rule changes, with known limits. Neither the probabilistic backend nor the scoped compiler was replaced.
ConstructionImplemented changeLimit
Source and prediction scopeKeep nominal content and its modifiers inside the image, assertion or prediction; nested evidence routes inherit their context.Event/nominal references inside these contexts remain incomplete.
Predicational is/wasFor “X is a/an NOUN,” put the noun description on the same referent as X.Bare mass-noun predication and equative clauses are not fully covered.
Location and noun phrasesExpose entity relations in spatial complements; preserve possessors, near/far-side modifiers and multiword names.Upstream attachment errors can still make a mast part of a location.
Embedded event subjectsUse an enhanced dependency subject link when available, then declared lexical classes for subject/object control.This is limited rule-based completion. Existing incorrect SRL roles are retained.
Participial and concessive clausesAttach participial descriptions to their nominal owner; retain “although” as a concessive relation.A malformed sentence or missing frame may still lose an event.

A controller is the participant understood as the subject of an embedded event. In “people watch a robot perform,” the robot performs. Added subject links are labeled nsubj or nsubj:pass; the code does not assume every subject is PropBank ARG0. ARG0 and ARG1 are predicate-specific role labels. A controller outside the declared classes can be refused rather than guessed.

Inspectable examples: Mississippi and its vessel registration, a robot performing, predicted rain, and Mare Crisium’s location. The damage shown in an image now retains depiction scope, but remains partial because the nominal damage and damaged event do not share an explicit identity link.

Meaning preservation: development and fresh review

Within-corpus development using cached trained SRL and dependency inputs. Twenty-four previously reviewed claims received manually specified complete reference readings and development inspection; ambiguous readings and a remaining event-nominal identity requirement are explicit. Twenty fresh IDs were sampled before changes, excluding earlier reviewed/pilot IDs. Compiler and reference hashes were frozen before fresh review and are checked. The same assistant judged original text and both graphs, without media/truth labels or independent human gold. This is not source-disjoint generalization or a corpus semantic accuracy estimate.

Faithful: meaning and principal bindings survive under the stated reading. Partial: some detail or relationship is opaque or missing. Incorrect: an actor, identity, assertion or scope changes. Does the logical program enforce the corrected binding?

We checked reference forms independently of automatic parsing. All 24 manually specified forms serialize as valid programs. For four development cases, constructed facts test both the parsed graph and the reference graph: matching facts should support the claim; misleading facts should refute it under the declared evidence policy. These are deterministic, point-mass checks, where each local distribution assigns probability one to a single state. They do not test learned or calibrated probabilities.

Native DeepProbLog: the robot and vessel negative checks finish with refuted probability 1 (reported backend times about 0.26 and 1.93 seconds). The nested image/damage check exceeds a 30-second subprocess limit that includes Python startup. Its independent logical check passes, but its native inference is not a pass. Whole-corpus inference, scaling and training throughput remain unmeasured.

The proposed steps are not completed. This bounded revision supports keeping one SRL/dependency compiler as the experimental frontend, with documented limits. It does not justify calling the compiler reliable yet. ADR-023 is unchanged; media verification and relation training remain deferred, and sentence rewriting remains unimplemented.
