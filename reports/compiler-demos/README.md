## Implemented changes

`compile_content_recovery` in `scripts/probabilistic_verification/content_recovery.py`
prepares copied annotations and transforms the copied logical form before the
existing compiler emits predicates. It never changes original claim text.

- **Appositions and participants:** Keep comma-separated qualifiers such as
  Kipnuk/Alaska or airport/Phoenix Arizona as explicit grammatical apposition.
  They are not hard-coded as identical entities. A noun conjunct attached through
  a person's name is reattached to its nominal participant, retaining Karen and
  the father as two group members. Earlier false-name/controller guards remain.
- **Numbers:** Keep source ranges, signs, bounds, units and approximation words.
  Roughly100km is a measurement with an above-Moon relation, not a group of100
  kilometers. From10-20 to25-30knots retains both endpoints and central Texas.
  At least120 keeps a lower bound and the100-officer subset. A case-marking
  including does not also become an unrelated verbal event. Times do not become
  counts, and explicit counted objects such as feet do not become distances.
- **Clauses:** Recover lands from a noun-tagging error only with a temporal marker,
  an adjacent singular participant and a verb-lexicon match. Repair finite clauses
  in supplied SRL time slots when the marker and subject are explicit. As remains
  temporal here, not an invented cause. As-long-as and other uncertain readings
  remain flagged. Finite clauses hidden in strings and names hidden in an
  undecomposed colon list now request structural review.
- **Sources and labels:** A timelapse owns depicted content. Sign/banner/poster
  assertions use OCR rather than speech; nested explicit source boundaries remain.
  Missing banner-read frames and restricted on-screen text fragments are recovered.
  A poster row stays exact printed text without assigning unprovided column
  meanings. Predicative a-Karen label text is separated from the person's name.

These reuse existing scoped propositions and typed arguments. Fixed or learned
verifiers must still score the resulting conditions. No new media model or learned
relation classifier was trained. The native demo executes the existing route policy
and DeepProbLog using stipulated OCR/speech observations; speech alone yields
unknown for printed text. No real poster or media clip was evaluated.

The old `compile_scope_coverage` gains an optional form-transform callback and
`run_scope_coverage_census.compile_one` an optional compiler argument. Their default
outputs exactly reproduce all2,375 preceding complete records. There is no
per-claim parser selection. Historical frozen sources and17 published ZIPs remain.
