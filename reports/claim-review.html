How to review

First describe what each original claim means without viewing the compiler outputs. Then compare two anonymous outputs against that reading. A nominal description can preserve meaning without being a complete sentence. Note ambiguity or missing context rather than inventing an entity, date or numeric threshold.

A and B are two compiler versions, in random order for each claim. Read the visible structures before rating them. “Participant 0/1/2” preserves verb-specific semantic roles; location, cause and time use named roles.

Faithful: the meaning and principal relationships survive. Partial: a detail or relationship is missing or opaque. Incorrect: an asserted meaning, participant or scope changes. Unsure: you cannot decide. If compilation was refused, assess whether that refusal is appropriate separately.

In the output trees, ARG0/ARG1 are predicate-specific semantic roles; nsubj/obj mean grammatical subject/object. A source context distinguishes depiction, sound, speech or on-screen text (OCR) from a world assertion. Node IDs identify shared participants/events. No media truth verdict is being judged here.
