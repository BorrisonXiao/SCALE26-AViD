The optimizer flattens associative AND/OR, removes identical repeated nodes, orders leaves by observation identity and builds balanced binary trees. It preserves NOT and evidence-combination boundaries. This combines balancing and ordering; their separate effects were not isolated. Mixed probabilities match an independent conjunction calculation, and repeated-neural-observation gradients match the previous encoding. Incomplete inventories and evidence conflicts are also checked.

The initial12-test run with the unbalanced full sufficiency check passed in 147.73 seconds. The expression change addresses cost; it does not certify the linguistic interpretation. Frozen earlier backends remain unchanged.
