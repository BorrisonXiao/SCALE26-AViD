Sample training through DeepProbLog

This is a training demonstration on two development claims from one video. One has the supported aircraft/bus colors; the other swaps the colors. Both labels come from assistant inspection. The same claims are shown before and after training. There is no held-out accuracy result.

The entity observations and logical rules are fixed. A 64-hidden-unit multilayer perceptron adjusts each attribute relation’s three probabilities. Its six inputs are the three frozen scores and their logarithms; it receives no claim IDs, candidate IDs, or target labels. It therefore acts mainly as a learned score calibrator in this first run.

For one claim, the loss is:

\[\mathcal{L}(\theta)=-\log p_{\theta}(y)\]

Here \(y\) is the reference claim label, \(p_{\theta}(y)\) is its probability returned by DeepProbLog, and \(\theta\) denotes the small classifier’s weights. Training averages this loss across claims. The reference labels supervise the final verdict; no local attribute labels are supplied to the training loop.

Corrections made during implementation

  • Event existence stays inside the scope of clause negation.
  • Participant variables are bound where their uses meet. A disjunction does not require candidates for an unused branch.
  • DeepProbLog 2.0.6’s near-zero/near-one simplification is overridden locally: only exact constants are identities. Double precision preserves very small probability outcomes and gradients.
  • Canonical observations are shared across duplicate predicates and alternative proofs.
  • Missing observations and incomplete candidate coverage yield explicit unknown states.

The probability tests compare DeepProbLog with an independent enumeration of categorical outcomes. Every displayed verdict distribution is checked to sum to one within one billionth, without renormalizing the backend output.

Current limits

  • Restricted English claims only. Quantified counts, unresolved pronouns, temporal/causal clauses, and source-assertion scope are rejected.
  • Real-media candidates are manually specified depicted referents; there is no automatic tracker in this demonstration.
  • The color relations are learned; the controlled event table only tests shared-event reasoning.
  • Perception scores are uncalibrated, and dependence between different model questions is not estimated.
  • No audio inference, multimodal-model fine-tuning, or held-out evaluation was run.
