Full-corpus results

Every original claim was processed. The earlier compiler and its outputs remain unchanged. The revised system compares trained semantic role labeling (SRL) with a frozen Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 text parser, then uses the same deterministic compiler. The text model reads the original claim. Its decoder limits output to the allowed JSON structure; this controls syntax, not meaning. A preliminary AMR-assisted neural condition was stopped after 128 outputs because it copied concepts absent from the original text and produced invalid structures. One bounded reparse is allowed for structural errors or selected diagnostic flags. Both attempts are retained. The final policy selects the executable SRL/neural candidate with fewer diagnostic penalties; ties favor SRL. Context needs remain explicit.

What changed

Table 4. Implemented changes. Scope and quantity predicates still need matching evidence functions before actual media verification.
ChangePrevious failureNew behavior
Clause arguments and source scopeEvent-valued arguments were rejected or sometimes treated as independent events.Reports, depictions, questions, reasons, purposes, beliefs, conditions and temporal clauses retain a parent and a separate context.
Noun-phrase relationsAn entire complex phrase could hide internal participants.Entities can be connected by explicit relations; groups can have named members. Parser omissions remain possible.
Quantities and literalsThe number guard rejected speeds, dates and counted groups.Group quantities retain an operator/value; speeds, durations, dates and text can be typed literals.
Pronouns and bare labelsPronouns were broadly refused; labels could become invented assertions.Keep reference requirements explicit. Standalone labels remain mentions.
Multiple sentences29 original claims had empty tokens under a one-sentence restriction.All sentences are retained, with offsets into the unchanged original text.
Validation and reparseAn invalid parse ended the attempt.Check IDs, mentions, anchors, quotes, argument types and unused entities. Allow one original-text reparse, without a sentence rewrite.

A scope identifies where a condition must be supported. For a narrator saying that a ship arrived, arrival is checked inside the narrator’s assertion. It is not an independent assertion that the ship arrived in the world. Scope IDs include the claim ID so that different source/modal contexts cannot accidentally share an evidence-cache entry. These IDs identify cached observations; they are not neural input features. A typed literal is a value such as 45 mph or exact quoted words; it is not a physical entity candidate. Quantities are group-level conditions. This is not a general theorem prover for arbitrary modal logic or universal quantification.

Meaning preservation review

One hundred claim IDs were sampled uniformly from all 2,375 original texts. These became development diagnostics: their review informed late compiler fixes. After freezing the compiler, twenty additional claim IDs, disjoint from those 100 and the initial 24-claim pilot, were sampled and reviewed against both old and new outputs. Two bugfixes followed that review: dead-declaration cleanup motivated by a development quote (all twenty program hashes unchanged), and claim-specific scope IDs to prevent different contexts sharing an evidence-cache entry. The latter changes raw program hashes but preserves every single-claim program after renaming scope IDs; this was checked on all accepted programs and all fresh compiled structures. Fresh statuses, diagnostics, selections and judgments are unchanged. The same assistant judged all cases from text and logical forms, without media/truth labels or independent human gold. Source videos can overlap; this is not a held-out generalization study. Fresh-case counts are descriptive checks, not a full-corpus accuracy estimate.

Execution checks and next decisions

All accepted programs were serialized and parsed by ProbLog. A deterministic 16-case sample restricted to at most 14 predicates, plus uncovered small scope/quantity/reference categories, was attempted with the actual DeepProbLog ExactEngine and a 15-second subprocess limit. An initial unrestricted-size sample was interrupted after a slow case. Every observation is deliberately unknown; no media verdict or full-corpus backend throughput is measured.

2 end-to-end native checks exceeded 15 seconds. This limit includes Python/model startup, so the result does not isolate solver cost. Whole-corpus inference and training throughput remain unmeasured.

  • Keep this compiler opt-in. Use the per-claim outputs for review rather than treating executable output as correct training data.
  • Write complete reference logical forms for the observed source, role, modifier and temporal failures, including correct refusals for unresolved fragments.
  • Evaluate a stronger semantic parser or targeted parser supervision against those references before broadening acceptance. The logical-form-to-DeepProbLog stage and natural-language interpretation need separate tests.
  • Supply context for pronouns/labels and define group evidence functions separately from compilation.
  • Keep media verification and relation-model training deferred until the compiled structures are reliable enough.
  • LLM sentence rewriting remains a reserved last resort and is not implemented.
