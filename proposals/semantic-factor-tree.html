Conjunct-local semantic factor trees

Probe content units independently, probe their bindings explicitly, and let messages—not contextualized questions—carry subtree evidence upward. The dependency parse remains useful as a scaffold, but the executable object is a typed semantic factor tree.

See the crowd example Read the motivating note

Status: deterministic compiler and gold-v3 annotation surface built. Selected text-model prompts are versioned; the local Qwen scale pass remains separately cacheable.

Decision

Local truths on nodes; bindings on factors

Recommended formulation

Every retained entity, event, or state receives an independent unary probe. Attributes and cardinalities become local observed factors on a host. Every retained parent–child connection is replaced by one typed relation factor over its immediate semantic endpoints. A latent, unprobed claim-output variable is computed recursively at the root.

This confirms the core interpretation developed in the discussion: there is no execution gate and no contextualized leaf question. A child probe is asked even when its parent is unsupported. The context-sensitive question is the edge probe—for example, whether this crowd participates in standing—and its conditional semantics live in the factor potential rather than in an English “given that…” preamble.

The resulting model gives up bottom-up text expansion, not bottom-up inference. Evidence still propagates from leaves to the root by exact belief propagation; what disappears is the practice of copying a child subtree into its parent’s emission.

Compiler contract

From dependency scaffold to factor tree

  1. Retain local truth variables.

    Keep concrete entities, events, and states. Retag reduced-clause gerunds conservatively—for example, noun-tagged standing under acl becomes an event.

  2. Contract grammatical material.

    Determiners, auxiliaries, case markers, punctuation, and similar operators do not become independent truth variables. Their text remains available as a relation marker.

  3. Attach local property factors.

    An attribute or cardinality scopes over its nearest retained host: attribute(crowd, Huge), not a unary “does Huge exist?” probe.

  4. Replace each retained connection with one factor.

    Core arguments use deterministic UD mappings where reliable; ambiguous acl, advcl, obl, nmod, and clausal complements go through a closed-label residual classifier.

  5. Add an unprobed output.

    A fixed root-composition factor connects the semantic root frontier to claim verdict. If a copular/property UD root was contracted, its predicate becomes an observed root factor instead.

Revised normalization

na is local and unconditional

The old normalization pass judged a contextualized unit such as standing together, so its decision mixed local checkability with how that phrase sat under its parent. In the new pass, the target span and optional host are fixed before the LLM is called. The decision is one of:

  • unary_probe for an independently testable entity, event, or state;
  • observed_factor for a property/cardinality on a supplied host;
  • contract for purely grammatical material;
  • abstain (the new precise meaning of na) only for unresolved referents, dangling fragments, or meanings without a stable local audio-visual truth value.

“The parent was not verified” is never a reason for abstain. Hard-to-see is not abstain. Names and numbers can be established by speech or readable text and remain probeable. This is intentionally different from a scene-graph pipeline that conditionally skips children.

Scope rule

Combine immediate endpoints, receive subtrees as messages

A relation factor combines only its immediate semantic arguments. For (f_{pc}), the model-generated observation may mention (p), (c), and the supplied relation marker—but not (p)’s other children or (c)’s descendants. The subtree still matters computationally:

\[ m_{c\rightarrow f_{pc}}(z_c) = \varphi_c(z_c) \prod_{g\in N(c)\setminus f_{pc}} m_{g\rightarrow c}(z_c). \]

The message (m_{c\rightarrow f_{pc}}) summarizes all evidence below (c). Thus the factor combines immediate nodes semantically and subtree beliefs probabilistically. Asking the factor question about both full subtrees would reintroduce the overlap the redesign is meant to remove.

Executable model

A bipartite tree preserves exact inference

Let (V) contain the local truth variables and latent output (y); let (F_{mathrm{local}}), (F_{mathrm{edge}}), and (F_{mathrm{fixed}}) contain property, binding, and grammatical/root factors. The score is

\[ P(z\mid x,c) \propto \prod_{v\in V_{\mathrm{probe}}}\varphi_v(z_v;x)\;\prod_{f\in F_{\mathrm{obs}}}\rho_f(z_{\partial f};x)\;\prod_{f\in F_{\mathrm{fixed}}}\psi_f(z_{\partial f}). \]

The compiler replaces every retained dependency connection with exactly one factor instead of laying factors on top of dependency edges. With one root-composition factor, the bipartite graph is connected and has (|E|=|V|+|F|-1); sum-product inference remains exact. The claim verdict is the marginal of (y), not a separate whole-claim MLLM emission.

Prompt engineering

Separate decisions, frozen selection rule, disjoint fixtures

Graph structure and language generation are deliberately separate. Unit normalization cannot alter edges; edge typing cannot invent units; question generation receives a fixed target and cannot change either. Each stage had a terse v1, constrained v2, and full locality-aware v3 candidate. Selection used a frozen score—80% behavioral correctness, 20% required-instruction coverage, shorter prompt on an exact tie.

The selected prompts and validated batch runner are implemented. At build time the Slurm controller was unreachable, so the local frozen Qwen pass could not launch. Gold-v3 therefore exposes deterministic parser/SRL decisions and question wording as fallback for annotation instead of silently exporting AViD claims to an external model. Model decisions will remain separate versioned caches when the local scheduler returns.

Risks and acceptance criteria

What annotation must settle

  • Event existence may be underspecified. “Does a standing event occur?” is local, but some verbs require a generic participant/object to sound natural. Question-quality labels quantify this.
  • Conditional binding is hard to elicit. A factor question must test identity or role rather than re-test endpoint existence. Binding-decoy cases are the primary stress test.
  • Parser topology can be wrong. The text model may type an offered edge but cannot invent a missing endpoint. Coverage failures need upstream reparsing, not prompt cleverness.
  • High-order roots need care. Contracted copular/cardinality roots become observed root factors; annotation should test whether these are understandable without a whole-claim question.

Adopt this factorization for the main experiment only if factor-role/relation agreement is high, most unary and binding questions are judged answerable without forbidden context, binding-decoy accuracy improves, and root verdicts remain calibrated under exact inference.
