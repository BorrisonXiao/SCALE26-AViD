Probabilistic parsing for multimodal claim verification

Compile a claim into probabilistic logic, use frozen visual and audio models to collect evidence, and learn small relation classifiers to check whether the participants and events are related as the claim says.

Read the chosen paper direction See generated examples Inspect meaning preservation in compilation Earlier tree-CRF note

Direction adopted 14 October 2026. A restricted compiler and sample DeepProbLog training run are implemented as of 22 October. Held-out method gains remain unmeasured. Earlier tree-CRF reports are retained below as historical evidence.

The idea in one paragraph

Represent the claim, score the relations, and infer support

Dependency parsing and semantic role labeling identify the claim's predicates and arguments. A compiler preserves their roles, shared variables, and logical scope in a probabilistic program. Fixed functions or small learned classifiers produce evidence probabilities from cached media-model outputs. Inference considers alternative evidence assignments and returns supported, refuted, or insufficient-evidence probabilities. Existing systems such as DeepProbLog can provide the inference and learning backend.
